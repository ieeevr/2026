<h1>Sudal-E: Our Eco-Friendly Conference Initiative</h1>
<p>“Sudal,” meaning otter in Korean, is the mascot of both the <strong>City of Daegu</strong> and <strong>IEEE VR 2026.</strong> The letter <strong>“E” stands for Environment</strong>, reflecting the conference’s commitment to sustainability. <br>
  <br>
  In the 1970s, Daegu’s rivers were severely polluted due to rapid industrial development. Through the efforts of the government and local citizens, the rivers were gradually restored, and <strong>otters—an important indicator of a healthy river ecosystem—returned to Daegu’s waterways.</strong> Inspired by this environmental recovery, IEEE VR 2026 honors the city’s symbol by promoting a <strong>more sustainable and environmentally responsible conference.</strong> <br>
  <br>
  To support this initiative, IEEE VR 2026 introduces <strong>“SuDal-E,” a social AR app experience for attendees.</strong> The application demonstrates how <strong>XR technology can raise environmental awareness</strong> through an interactive mission in which participants rescue endangered otters, clean polluted habitats, and build their own sanctuary. <br>
  <br>
  The conference also implements several <strong>sustainable practices.</strong> Conference bags are made from <strong>recycled materials,</strong> and <strong>reusable cups</strong> are provided for participants. A <strong>cup-washing station</strong> is available in the coffee break area at<strong> Grand Ballroom A (3F), EXCO,</strong> encouraging participants to reuse their cups throughout the event. </p>
